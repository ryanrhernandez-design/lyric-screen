Lyric Screen

Leave this page open and it shows the lyrics, in time, for whatever is playing on your Spotify account.

Spotify login only works on a secure address. Open this page over https://, or on the device itself at http://127.0.0.1.

This button works for accounts the site owner has added. Anyone else can connect with their own Spotify app below.

Connect with your own Spotify app
  1. Open the Spotify Developer Dashboard and choose Create app. Spotify requires a Premium account for this.
  2. Under Redirect URIs add exactly this address, and tick Web API:
  3. Save, open the app's settings, and paste its Client ID here:

Your login is kept only in this browser, so this device stays connected until you log out. Spotify asks you to reconnect about every six months. The page talks directly to Spotify, and sends the song title and artist to LRCLIB to find lyrics. There is no other server.
